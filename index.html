About HoverScreen

Cinema belongs
to everyone.

HoverScreen was born from a simple belief: language and hearing loss should never stand between a person and the magic of cinema.

Our story

From frustration
to invention

France welcomes over 102 million tourists every year — the highest of any country in the world. Yet most of them can't follow a film in a local cinema. Meanwhile, 1 in 4 French adults live with some degree of hearing impairment. Traditional subtitle solutions force the eyes down, breaking the immersion that makes cinema so powerful.

HoverScreen started as a student project — a question: what if subtitles moved with you instead of sitting fixed at the bottom of a screen? Two years of prototyping, sensor testing, and AI integration later, we have an answer.

We are proud to be building a French tech company at the intersection of culture, accessibility, and innovation — supported by France 2030 and aligned with l'exception culturelle.
